GTAIV-FastGPS 1.0.0

Requires GTA IV Complete Edition 1.2.0.59 and a 32-bit ASI loader.
Extract FastGPS.asi and FastGPS.ini beside GTAIV.exe or into your ASI loader's plugin folder. Remove any older FastGPS ASI and restart the game.
To uninstall, remove both files. Logging is off by default.

Source, configuration guide and support:
https://github.com/TheCraZyDuDee/GTAIV-FastGPS
